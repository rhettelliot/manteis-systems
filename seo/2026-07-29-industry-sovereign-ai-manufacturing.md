## The Manufacturing IP Problem

Manufacturing runs on intellectual property. Product designs, process documentation, supplier relationships, quality data, and pricing structures are the competitive moat. The moment you feed that data to a cloud AI API, you've introduced a third party into your most sensitive operations.

The risks are existential:

- **Product design IP** — Cloud AI vendors' training data policies are opaque. Your CAD files, spec sheets, and design iterations could inform models that later serve your competitors.
- **Process data** — Yield rates, defect patterns, and process parameters are trade secrets. Cloud processing creates a disclosure risk.
- **Supplier relationships** — Pricing, lead times, and supplier performance data in a cloud AI creates competitive intelligence leakage.
- **Operational continuity** — Factory floors can't depend on internet connectivity to a cloud AI endpoint. Latency, outages, and bandwidth constraints make cloud AI impractical for real-time manufacturing.
- **IT/OT convergence risk** — Connecting Operational Technology networks to cloud services expands the attack surface dramatically.
