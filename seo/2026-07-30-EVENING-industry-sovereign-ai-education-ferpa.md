## Why Education Needs Sovereign AI

### The FERPA Trap

FERPA protects "personally identifiable information" in education records. When a teacher types a student's name, grades, behavioral notes, or disability accommodations into a cloud AI service, that data leaves the institution's control. Under FERPA:

- Schools cannot disclose PII from education records without consent — but cloud AI terms of service often constitute disclosure
- Schools are responsible for third-party vendors' handling of FERPA-protected data
- Cloud AI providers' data retention policies frequently conflict with FERPA's destruction requirements
- Model training pipelines may inadvertently retain and regurgitate student data

**Sovereign AI eliminates this entire risk class.** When the model runs on your server, in your data center, behind your firewall — no data ever leaves the institution. FERPA compliance is architectural, not contractual.

## FERPA Compliance Architecture

### How Manteis Sovereign AI Satisfies FERPA Requirements

**1. No Disclosure to Third Parties**

FERPA §99.31 prohibits disclosure of PII from education records without consent. Sovereign AI processes all data on institutional infrastructure — no disclosure to any third party occurs. The AI model, the inference engine, and the data storage all reside on hardware the institution controls.

**2. Data Destruction Requirements**

FERPA requires that institutions can ensure destruction of education records held by third parties. With sovereign AI, destruction is trivial: delete the local database, wipe the storage, and the data is gone. No reliance on a cloud provider's deletion API that may or may not actually remove data from their training pipelines.

**3. Audit Trail Requirements**

Manteis Fortress includes full audit logging: every query, every data access, every model invocation is timestamped, user-attributed, and retention-managed. This satisfies FERPA's record-keeping requirements and provides the documentation needed for compliance audits.

**4. Access Control**

Role-based access control ensures only authorized personnel can access FERPA-protected data processed by the AI system. Special education teachers see only their students' data. Counselors see only their caseload. Administrators see only aggregate, de-identified summaries.
