Cloud AI costs scale linearly with usage. An **AI appliance** has a fixed cost. After the break-even point — typically 8–14 months — every additional query is effectively free.

### The Sovereignty Argument

When you use cloud AI, your data travels to a third-party data center, is processed by a model you don't control, and may be retained for training. When you use an **AI in a box** appliance:

- Your data never leaves your network perimeter
- The model weights are physically on your hardware
- You control when (or if) updates happen
- No vendor can deprecate your model or change your pricing
- No subpoena can access your inference logs without going through you

### The Compliance Argument

For HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI-DSS, ITAR, and CMMC environments, cloud AI introduces a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) surface and shared-responsibility complexity. An on-premise **sovereign AI appliance** eliminates the third-party data processor entirely. The compliance boundary is your building.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a data center to run an AI appliance?

**Manteis One** runs on a desk in a standard office. **Manteis Core** needs a 2U rack slot and standard power. **Manteis Fortress** requires a 4U rack slot, 1500W power, and climate-controlled environment. None require specialized cooling beyond standard office HVAC.

### Can I upgrade the hardware later?

Yes. All three appliances use standard, user-replaceable components. You can add GPUs, RAM, and NVMe storage as your needs grow. The Manteis Sovereign OS automatically detects new hardware and rebalances model serving.

### What if a model gets updated — do I need internet?

Model updates are optional, not mandatory. You can download new models via a one-time internet connection (or USB transfer for air-gapped environments). Your existing models continue running indefinitely without any internet access.

### How does this compare to buying a Dell server and installing Ollama myself?

You absolutely can. The Manteis appliance saves you the engineering time: hardware selection, driver conflicts, model quantization, OS configuration, security hardening, and deployment documentation. For a team without a dedicated ML engineer, the appliance is the difference between 2 weeks and 2 months to production.

### Can I try sovereign AI before buying an appliance?

Yes. The [Sovereign AI Starter Kit](https://manteis.systems/starter-kit) ($97) is a deployment blueprint that walks you through building a sovereign AI system on hardware you already own. If you outgrow it, the appliance is the natural upgrade.
