## THE FRAMEWORK — 5 QUESTIONS IN 30 MINUTES

### Question 1: DATA SOVEREIGNTY (5 min)
"Can your data leave your network? Are there regulatory, contractual, or competitive reasons you can't use cloud AI?"

- YES (regulated) → Perfect fit. Sovereign AI is the only option. Lead with Phase 1.
- PARTIAL (some data sensitive) → Good fit. Hybrid architecture possible. Lead with Phase 1.
- NO (no restrictions) → Still viable — sell on cost advantage and infrastructure ownership. Lead with kit + Phase 1.

### Question 2: MANUAL PROCESS PAIN (5 min)
"What tasks are your staff doing manually that take hours but feel like they should take minutes?"

Listen for:
- Document generation/proccessing → Phase 1 (Business Process Automation)
- IT support/ticket triage → Phase 2 (Autonomous IT Operations)
- Security monitoring → Phase 3 (Autonomous Security Operations)
- Infrastructure headaches (printers, etc) → Phase 4 (Proactive Infrastructure)
- Legacy ERP frustration → Phase 5 (AI-Powered ERP Tooling)

### Question 3: INFRASTRUCTURE READINESS (5 min)
"Do you have hardware that could run AI workloads? A server, a desktop with decent specs, or budget for a $2-5K workstation?"

- Has hardware → Good. We can deploy on existing infrastructure.
- Needs hardware → Include hardware spec in proposal. $2-5K for a capable inference workstation.
- Cloud-only currently → Perfect opportunity to migrate to sovereign. Cost comparison is the pitch.

### Question 4: TEAM CAPABILITY (5 min)
"Does your IT team have experience with Docker, command line, or workflow automation tools?"

- Strong IT team → Full deployment, train them to maintain.
- Basic IT → Full deployment + extended training. n8n visual builder is their entry point.
- No IT team → Managed retainer model. We deploy and maintain.

### Question 5: TIMELINE & BUDGET (5 min)
"If this made sense for your environment, what timeline and budget range would you be working with?"

- $15-25K available → Phase 1 only. Perfect entry point.
- $25-50K available → Phase 1 + Phase 2 or Phase 3. Multi-phase proposal.
- $50K+ available → Full roadmap. Discounted multi-phase engagement.
- Unclear → Send the $97 kit. Let them evaluate. Follow up in 2 weeks.

---

## THE CLOSE (5 min)

Based on the answers, recommend the specific phase(s) that address their pain. If the fit is clear:

"Based on what you've told me, Phase 1 [specific name] would address [specific pain point]. The engagement is [price range], takes [timeline], and at the end your team owns the system. I can have a written proposal to you by [date]. Should I draft that up?"

If the fit is uncertain:
"I'll send you the Sovereign AI Starter Kit — it's the complete deployment blueprint at $97. Read through it, and if it resonates, we can talk about a Phase 1 engagement. No pressure either way."
