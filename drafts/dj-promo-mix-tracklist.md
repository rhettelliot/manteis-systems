## Mix Concept

**Title:** "Sovereign Frequencies — A Manteis Recordings Journey"
**Format:** 60-minute continuous mix
**Genre:** Deep textural electronic / ambient techno / experimental
**Vibe:** A descent from light to deep — starting with melodic warmth, descending into textural abstraction, resolving in hypnotic drone. The arc mirrors the Sovereign OS philosophy: from surface to depth, from cloud to local, from rented to owned.

## Transition Notes

1. **Track 1 → 2:** Crossfade at 5:00, layer Foundations' kick under GMGFE's tail for 30s
2. **Track 2 → 3:** Beatmatch exit — filter Foundations down, let Ataraxia's pad swell in
3. **Track 3 → 4:** Hard cut on the drop — Ataraxia's tension resolves as Continuous's groove hits
4. **Track 4 → 5:** Long crossfade (2 min) — Continuous's rhythm dissolves into The Source's textures
5. **Track 5 → 6:** Ambient crossfade — no beat, pure texture blend, 90 seconds of overlap
6. **Track 6 → 7:** Granular transition — sample Upekṣā's tail, reverse it, feed into Phoneme's opening
7. **Track 7 → 8:** Drone merge — both tracks play simultaneously for 2 min, Phoneme fades, Deep Field Image takes over
8. **Track 8 → 9:** Slow crossfade — Deep Field Image's cosmic drone gives way to Violet Cirrus's warmth
9. **Track 9 → end:** Fade to silence over 60s, leave 10s of pure silence before track ends
