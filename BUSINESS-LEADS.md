- **Follow-up message (after connection):** "Thanks for connecting, [NAME]. We recently helped a 200-person PNW firm cut manual operations by 70% with local AI agents — zero cloud spend, full data sovereignty. If [COMPANY] is exploring AI or security initiatives, I'd love to do a free 30-min sovereignty audit of your current setup. No pitch deck, just a real conversation."

## Key Notes

- No pricing in initial outreach — sell the conversation, not the service
- Lead with the sovereignty audit (free 30-min consult)
- PNW companies value local relationships — emphasize Seattle presence
- LinkedIn is the primary channel for B2B in Seattle tech
- Follow up within 1 week of connection, then drop it
- Don't cold email — Seattle tech culture is LinkedIn-first
- Reference the case study (42-tool AI agent deployment) once it's published
