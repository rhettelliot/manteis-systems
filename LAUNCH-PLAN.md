**Monetization:**
- Stripe or Lemon Squeezy for subscriptions

**Why Stripe vs Lemon Squeezy:**
- Stripe: better for B2B, invoicing, tax, global payments, subscription management
- Lemon Squeezy: better for digital products, simpler setup, built-in tax for SaaS
- **Recommendation:** Stripe for consulting/hardware, Lemon Squeezy for cloud SaaS subscriptions
