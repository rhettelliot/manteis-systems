## TOP 3 ACTIONABLE TARGETS

### 1. Musicbed
**Submission URL / contact:** `support@musicbed.com` (per Musicbed FAQ: *“How can I submit my music to be on Musicbed?”*)
- Live FAQ page: https://support.musicbed.com/musicbed-faq/how-can-i-submit-my-music-to-be-on-musicbed
- Main site: https://www.musicbed.com

**Genres/moods they actively seek:**
- Musicbed’s catalog explicitly carries ambient, cinematic, electronic, dark electronic, synthwave-friendly, singer-songwriter/alt-vocal. Verified live artist examples include Alaskan Tapes (Ambient/Cinematic/Indie/Classical/Electronic), AK (Electronic/Ambient/Cinematic/Hip Hop/Pop), Ryan Taubert (Cinematic/Alternative/Electronic), and Joshua Crispin / Generdyn (Cinematic/Electronic/Trailer).
- Search filters include Genre, Mood, Artist, Instrument, Lyrical / Instrumental — works well for instrumental textural/electronic cues.

**Submission requirements (from public pages):**
- No public upload portal; Musicbed requests artists email `support@musicbed.com` to learn more about the submission process.
- Need fully cleared, original music (must own or control master + publishing rights).
- They offer subscription and single-song (micro) licenses, plus traditional custom licensing for bigger placements.
- A 50% royalty split is commonly cited for Musicbed artists (not explicitly confirmed on site, but consistent with public industry reporting). Verify when you email.

**Non-exclusive?**
- Musicbed licenses are non-exclusive from the *licensee/buyer* side; artists generally license music to Musicbed on a non-exclusive basis but you should confirm exact artist terms in your email.

**Upfront fees:**
- Free to apply / submit via email. No mention of submission fees.

**Typical sync fee range:**
- Micro/single-song licenses: roughly **$49–$500** for smaller web/podcast/personal projects.
- Custom/traditional licenses: no set price; negotiated based on viewership, distribution, brand size, etc. The FAQ says traditional licensing fees are market-driven and can run much higher for large brand campaigns.
- Custom music production budget also available via https://www.musicbed.com/custom-music.

**Best pitch angle for Manteis:**
- Frame Manteis as a curated deep-textural electronic label with 9 releases / 5 artists across ambient, dark electronic, cinematic electronic, synthwave, techno/DnB, spiritual drone and alt-vocal.
- Reference Musicbed’s existing ambient/electronic roster and ask to add catalog-wide placements for film, trailers, ads and podcasts.

### 2. Marmoset Music
**Submission URL / contact:**
- Main site: https://www.marmosetmusic.com
- Contact form: https://www.marmosetmusic.com/contact
- Artist page: https://www.marmosetmusic.com/about/artists
- Licensing/creative inquiries: `creative@marmosetmusic.com` (shown in license agreement)
- General support: `support@marmosetmusic.com`

**Genres/moods they actively seek:**
- Marmoset’s live roster and featured producers list heavily emphasizes **Cinematic, Ambient, Electronic, Alternative, Sound Design, Trailer, Folk, Indie, Singer-Songwriter**.
- Featured live examples: Chris Coleman (Cinematic, Ambient), Hannah Parrott (Cinematic, Trailer, Sound Design, Ambient), Ryan Taubert (Cinematic, Alternative, Electronic), Joshua Crispin / Generdyn (Cinematic, Electronic, Trailer), Dustin Lau (Cinematic, Ambient, Indie), Luke Atencio (Cinematic), Zane Callister & Jared Logan / Utah (Beat-Driven, Hip Hop, Electronic).
- Their core pitch is “authentic music curated with your films in mind” — ideal for the textural/cinematic side of the Manteis catalog.

**Submission requirements:**
- No public artist application form found. Best approach: use the Contact form at `/contact` or email `creative@marmosetmusic.com` / `support@marmosetmusic.com` with a short pitch, private streaming links (SoundCloud / Bandcamp / Spotify), and download links for WAVs.
- Marmoset states it is a community-first platform that has paid artists >$44M and emphasizes artist equity / transparency.
- Standard Marmoset licenses are non-exclusive, single-purpose, perpetual click-through licenses. Artist-side contract should be confirmed by their team.

**Non-exclusive?**
- Buyer-side licenses are non-exclusive. Artist deals are typically non-exclusive, but confirm exact terms.

**Upfront fees:**
- Free to contact / submit. No submission fees visible.

**Typical sync fee range (from published license agreement, https://www.marmosetmusic.com/license-agreement):**
- Individual Creator: $69
- Student: $49
- Wedding: $99
- Podcast single use: $85 / series use: $260
- Independent Film: $299
- Small Business web: $315–$750 depending on client size
- Non-Profit: $150–$365
- Event: $55–$155
- Larger brand / broadcast / advertising / trailer placements are negotiated as custom licenses.
- Many of Manteis’ target placements will fall in the **$250–$50K** range via custom quotes.

**Best pitch angle for Manteis:**
- Reference Marmoset’s stated artist philosophy and its ambient/cinematic/electronic roster.
- Position Manteis as a boutique label supplying deep-textural electronic music for film, trailers, podcasts and brand campaigns.
- Offer to send a curated playlist of 10–15 tracks with proper metadata and split sheets ready.

- When open, they require artists who are **not affiliated with collecting societies/PROs** and work on a **non-exclusive** basis.
- Epidemic owns all rights to tracks (direct licensing model) and pays:

**Non-exclusive?**

**Upfront fees:**
- Free to apply, but the application portal is closed.

**Typical sync fee range:**
- Epidemic is a subscription library; artists do not set per-sync fees. Artist revenue comes from upfront track fees + streaming royalties + soundtrack/AI bonuses.

**Action item:**
- Bookmark and check back. In the meantime, a soft introduction email to `hello@epidemicsound.com` noting Manteis’ catalog and asking when artist applications will reopen is a low-effort future-proof move.
