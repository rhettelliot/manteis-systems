## TIER 1 — Primary Targets (Electronic/DJ-friendly, active booking)

### 1. Kremwerk + Timbre Room
- **Address:** 1809 Minor Ave, Seattle, WA 98101 (Downtown)
- **Capacity:** ~300 (Kremwerk), ~100 (Timbre Room)
- **Vibe:** Seattle's premier underground electronic music venue. Techno, house, experimental. Dark room, Funktion-One sound. The Timbre Room is the smaller, more intimate annex.
- **Booking:** booking@kremwerk.com — weekly residencies, touring acts, local openers
- **Why Manteis:** This is the exact scene. Deep textural electronic music fits the Kremwerk aesthetic. Pitch as a local opener or mid-week residency.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — check their calendar for open slots

### 2. Monkey Loft
- **Address:** 5005 25th Ave NE, Seattle, WA 98105 (U District)
- **Capacity:** ~250
- **Vibe:** Loft-style venue with a rooftop. House, techno, disco, ambient. Community-driven, inclusive atmosphere. One of the best sound systems in the city for electronic music.
- **Booking:** Contact via website form (monkeyloft.com) or Facebook
- **Why Manteis:** The loft aesthetic aligns with the Manteis Recordings brand — intimate, textural, intentional. Ambient/deep sets would land here.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — strong fit

### 3. The Underground
- **Address:** 222 N 85th St, Seattle, WA 98103 (Greenwood)
- **Capacity:** ~200
- **Vibe:** Underground electronic music focus. Techno, house, bass music. 21+, late-night events. Consistently books local electronic artists.
- **Booking:** theundergroundseattle@gmail.com
- **Why Manteis:** Direct fit for Manteis Recordings sound. Local artist-friendly booking.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — prime target

### 4. Re-bar
- **Address:** 1804 Howell St, Seattle, WA 98101 (Downtown/Denny Regrade)
- **Capacity:** ~250
- **Vibe:** Seattle institution. Multi-genre but regularly hosts electronic nights, techno, house, experimental. Queer-friendly, progressive, art-forward. Has been hosting electronic events since the 90s.
- **Booking:** rebarsounds@gmail.com
- **Why Manteis:** Historic venue, electronic music pedigree. Pitch as a Manteis Recordings showcase night — curated lineup with 2-3 artists.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — legacy venue, bookable

### 5. Neumos
- **Address:** 925 E Pike St, Seattle, WA 98122 (Capitol Hill)
- **Capacity:** ~500 (main room), ~100 (Barboza)
- **Vibe:** Mid-size live venue. Books electronic acts, touring DJs, live electronic performers. Barboza is the attached smaller room — more intimate, electronic/DJ-focused.
- **Booking:** booking@neumos.com
- **Why Manteis:** Step-up venue for when Manteis builds draw. Barboza is the realistic first target — smaller room, electronic-friendly.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — Barboza first, Neumos main room later

## TIER 2 — Strong Fits (Electronic-adjacent, curated nights)

### 6. Lo-Fi Performance Gallery
- **Address:** 429 Eastlake Ave E, Seattle, WA 98109 (Eastlake)
- **Capacity:** ~150
- **Vibe:** Art gallery + performance space. Ambient, experimental, electronic, jazz, sound art. Curated events, not a standard club. Perfect for listening-room style electronic performances.
- **Booking:** info@lofiproject.com
- **Why Manteis:** The most aesthetically aligned venue on this list. Deep textural electronic music in a gallery setting is the exact Manteis Recordings positioning. Pitch a Manteis Recordings listening night.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — highest aesthetic match

### 7. Crocodile
- **Address:** 2200 2nd Ave, Seattle, WA 98121 (Belltown)
- **Capacity:** ~500 (main), ~120 (Back Bar)
- **Vibe:** Legendary Seattle rock venue that has expanded into electronic booking. The Back Bar hosts smaller, more experimental nights. Touring electronic acts play the main room.
- **Booking:** booking@thecrocodile.com
- **Why Manteis:** The Back Bar is the entry point. Build a relationship through the smaller room before pitching the main stage.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — Back Bar focus

### 8. Capitol Hill Block Party / Neighbors
- **Address:** 1709 E Pike St, Seattle, WA 98102 (Capitol Hill)
- **Capacity:** ~300 (Neighbors)
- **Vibe:** Neighbors is a long-running Capitol Hill venue. Electronic music nights, drag shows, dance parties. The Block Party is the annual outdoor festival (July) that books electronic acts on side stages.
- **Booking:** info@neighborsseattle.com
- **Why Manteis:** Neighbors for regular booking; Block Party for festival exposure once Manteis builds a local following.
- **Status:** ACTIVE — Neighbors for dance-floor-oriented sets

## TIER 3 — Festival & Special Event Targets

### 9. Bumbershoot
- **Address:** Seattle Center (multiple stages)
- **Capacity:** 10,000+ (festival)
- **Vibe:** Seattle's major music and arts festival (Labor Day weekend). Books electronic acts across multiple stages. Returns in 2026 after restructuring.
- **Booking:** Submission via bumbershoot.com when application window opens (typically 6-8 months before event)
- **Why Manteis:** Festival exposure — long-term goal. Apply once Manteis Recordings has 10+ releases and proven local draw.
- **Status:** FUTURE — apply for 2027

### 10. Decibel Festival / Selectors
- **Address:** Various venues across Seattle
- **Capacity:** Multi-venue (500-2,000 per event)
- **Vibe:** Decibel was Seattle's premier electronic music festival (2003-2019). Selectors is the successor event series — curated electronic music across multiple venues. The gold standard for electronic music curation in the Pacific Northwest.
- **Booking:** Newt Briggs / selectorseattle.com — typically curates from established artists with releases and touring history
- **Why Manteis:** The dream booking. Requires 10+ releases, proven live performance history, and industry relationships. Build toward this over 2026-2027.
- **Status:** FUTURE — 2027 target once catalog and draw established
